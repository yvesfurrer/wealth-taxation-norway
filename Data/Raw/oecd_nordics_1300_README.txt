oecd_nordics_1300.csv: OECD Revenue Statistics category 1300 (taxes on income, profits and capital gains unallocable between 1100 and 1200), percent of GDP, DNK, FIN, ISL, NOR, SWE, 2010 to 2024.
Source: OECD Data Explorer, DF_RSOECD, accessed 30 September 2026 (SDMX generic XML, year and value from the same Obs element):
https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.CTP.TPS,DSD_REV_COMP_OECD@DF_RSOECD,2.0/DNK+FIN+ISL+NOR+SWE.TAX_REV.S13.T_1300._T.PT_B1GQ.A?startPeriod=2010&endPeriod=2024
Content: Denmark = pension yield tax (PAL-skat), Iceland = a smaller item. Finland, Norway, and Sweden report 0 in all years.
Use: Section 1 of Masterarbeit.R (columns pct_1300 and y_main_ex1300 in the panel), Section 7 (control-side variant ex-1300, Table A1.5 and Table A1.8, Panel B).
